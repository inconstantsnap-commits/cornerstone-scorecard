The fruit of the Spirit

Galatians 5:22–23 — read these out before you start, so everyone knows what each fruit means.

Love — caring for others, putting them first

Joy — deep gladness, even when things are hard

Peace — calm and settled, not anxious, not at war

Patience — waiting and enduring, without giving up

Kindness — being good to others, care in action

Goodness — doing what is right, and doing it generously

Faithfulness — staying true: loyal, steady, keeps promises

Gentleness — strength under control, not harsh

Self-Control — ruling yourself, not ruled by urges

The four stages

The columns. Every connection moves through these stages, left to right — read these out too, before anyone marks a circle.

Called — starting out: the connection begins, you know of each other

Known — you actually know each other: names, needs, real faces, not just a contact

Knit — tied together: you work together, and trust is there

Sent — going out together: the connection turns outward, on mission, instead of just existing between you

So for each fruit, you mark how it stands at each stage: is Love connected when you are just Called? When you are Known? When you are Knit? When you are Sent?

How a church uses this

1. Hand out the paper Cornerstone Scorecard, or pass one phone around the room.

2. Enter each person's card here — their fruit, the fruit they serve with, and their thirty-six chips.

3. Open Combined and read the picture together: where the Body is connected, where it needs help, and where it is disconnected.

4. Meeting in several rooms? Each room keeps its own collection, then one person imports them all into one device for the whole-church picture.
