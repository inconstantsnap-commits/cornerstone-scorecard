# The Cornerstone Scorecard

**Your strength is in the Body.** A free tool to help a church see how it is connected — many members, one Body (after 1 Corinthians 12).

## Use it

📱 **Open the app:** https://inconstantsnap-commits.github.io/cornerstone-scorecard/ — that page asks one question: which copy is yours?

It works in any phone or computer browser — no account, no sign-up, nothing to install.

⬇ **Or download a copy:** save `pastor.html` or `congregation.html` from this repo to your device and open it. Each copy is one single file, and it works offline. (Tip: export a JSON backup now and then — your collection lives on your device.)

## Two copies: pastor & congregation

The tool comes in two copies, so the congregation is not overburdened with the data and the stats:

- **Pastor copy** (`pastor.html`) — the full picture. Everything: all scorecards, the combined wheel and grid **with** percentages and counts, Talk it through with the room's numbers, comparing two gatherings, collection filters, JSON/CSV export and import, printing, and erasing.
- **Congregation copy** (`congregation.html`) — just your card. Enter your own scorecard, see your cards on the device, and view the combined picture in plain words: the wheel and the coloured grid (no percentages, no counts underneath), where we are strongest, where we need each other most, and the two Talk-it-through questions. One simple button hands your scorecards to your pastor as a file — nothing else to manage.

On the same device the two copies share one collection, so cards entered in the congregation copy appear in the pastor copy automatically.

## How it works

1. Each person fills in the Cornerstone Scorecard: the household they belong to, the household their body exists to serve (the star), and a chip — **Connected (Faithful)**, **Needs Help (Growing)**, or **Disconnected (Scattered)** — for every connection on the board.
2. Enter each person's card into the tool (or pass one phone around the room).
3. Open **Combined** to see the whole church on one picture: a wheel of every connection, a grid of circles (a dotted ring means the room is split on that circle), and a **Talk it through** panel — pick any household to see how the room marked it and get the two questions that start the conversation: *What does each side know that the other doesn't?* and *Who could move one rating in the next 90 days?*
4. Several rooms or several churches? Each keeps its own collection, then one person imports them all into a single device for the full picture (**Share** tab: JSON export/import; CSV for spreadsheets).

## The households

| | |
|---|---|
| **Tentmakers** | Entrepreneurs — people who start or run a business |
| **Great Households** | Corporates — big companies and employers |
| **Rulers & Magistrates** | Government — officials and public leaders |
| **Scribes & Psalmists** | Storytellers — writers, artists, and musicians |
| **The Young** | Youth — young people and new talent |
| **Deacons** | Service providers — people who help and serve others |
| **Guilds & Craftsmen** | Trade & industry — skilled workers and makers |
| **Assemblies & Churches** | Support — churches and groups that hold others up |
| **Talents & Tithes** | Money — givers and funders |
| **God** | Governance — God, who holds it all together |

The four stages of every connection: **Called** (starting out) → **Known** (know each other) → **Knit** (work together) → **Sent** (go out together).

## Collection codes — many groups, one tool

Every scorecard is stamped with a **collection code**, so gatherings stay sorted when several groups use the tool over time.

- Before a gathering, set a code in the app (type your own, like `IMMANUEL-OCT26`, or tap **Generate one**). Everyone entering cards at that gathering uses the **same code on every phone** — the code travels inside every exported file.
- **People** and **Combined** can be filtered to a single collection, or show everything at once.
- With two or more collections on a device, **Combined → Compare two gatherings** shows how the picture changed: overall Connected / Needs Help / Disconnected, and which threads moved up or slipped back.
- A coordinator can hand off or receive **one collection at a time** (Share tab → "Download this collection only"), so churches keep their own data and share only what they mean to.
