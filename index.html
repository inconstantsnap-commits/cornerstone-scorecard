📖 Pastor copy — the full picture

For pastors and leaders. Gather everyone's scorecards, see the whole church on one picture with the numbers: percentages, counts, comparisons between gatherings, and bringing several devices' collections together.

🙏 Congregation copy — just your card

For everyone else. Fill in your own scorecard, and see the picture in plain words. No percentages, no counts, no data to manage — nothing to be overburdened with.

Open the congregation copy

Good to know

Both copies are free, and each one is a single file that works offline once downloaded. On the same device, the two copies share one collection — cards entered in the congregation copy show up in the pastor copy, and the other way around.
