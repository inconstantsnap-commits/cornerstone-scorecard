How a church uses this

1. Hand out the paper Cornerstone Scorecard, or pass one phone around the room.

2. Enter each person's card here — their household, the household they serve, and their forty chips.

3. Open Combined and read the picture together: where the Body is faithful, where it is growing, and where it is scattered.

4. Meeting in several rooms? Each room keeps its own collection, then one person imports them all into one device for the whole-church picture.
